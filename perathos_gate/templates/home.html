Approval notifications

This device can show a notification when an agent in your organisation asks for approval. First confirm it is you with your approval passkey, then enable notifications.

On iPhone and iPad, notifications only work from the Home Screen. Do this once:

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up). On newer versions it is behind the … button.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Perathos Gate from the new Home Screen icon and follow the steps there.
